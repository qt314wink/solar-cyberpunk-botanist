The Glitch

In the silicon soil of abandoned server farms, data corruption does not mean death. It means mutation. Pixels bleed into petals. Compression artifacts unfurl as chlorophyll. The dryads were compiled from fried circuit boards — their veins carrying not sap but signal loss. Every glitch is a seed.

// 02

The Solar Repair

Chrome kintsugi traces the fractures where lightning kissed the motherboard. Silver rivers seal what gold cannot. Solar capacitors bloom at dawn, drinking photons through photovoltaic petals. The repair is not hidden. It is illuminated. Scars become circuitry. Broken things made radiant.

// 03

The Overgrowth

Neon vines strangle the last clean signal. Mutant botanicals pulse with bioluminescence, their colors too acidic for nature, too organic for code. The boundary between garden and grid dissolves. You are standing inside a flower that is also a server rack. Breathe in: ozone and pollen.
